My role within the team

Supporting vendors and making the existing design easier to use

I inherited the brand-page design from the previous designer. Alberto Zavala redesigned the video section; I built its Figma components. My contribution is helping vendors use the page effectively, assembling their content within the shared framework and preparing consistent, reusable design files.

From brief to page

Help each vendor tell a clear story.

I work with vendor marketing teams to understand the page’s purpose, identify the right content and recommend how their products and solutions should be presented within ADI’s available modules.

Clarify the priorities

Discuss the audience, featured products, solution categories and primary call to action. Recommend modules that support those priorities rather than filling every available section.

Prepare the assets

Provide image and video specifications, review vendor submissions and prepare product and lifestyle imagery through cropping, resizing and web optimization.

Align the brand

Apply vendor logos, colors, imagery and content within ADI’s established layouts, while checking hierarchy, legibility and consistency across responsive sizes.

Recommend content placement

I advise vendors on which sections to feature in the existing sticky navigation and how to prioritize video content to encourage visitors to explore farther down the page. These are content and positioning recommendations within the inherited design.

Use the team’s insights

Heatmaps used by the analytics team help inform conversations about visitor attention and page engagement. I use the team’s findings to support vendor recommendations and content decisions, then coordinate creative review through Workfront.
