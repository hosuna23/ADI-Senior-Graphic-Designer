Email design

Brand stories and promotions, ready for the inbox

Email creative for ADI Global Distribution, combining vendor branding, lifestyle and product imagery, promotional offers and clear calls to action. I design the visual hierarchy, prepare the imagery and build campaign content in Marketo.

Design approach

Make the offer easy to understand.

Requests often combine vendor awareness with a product or discount promotion. I establish the order of information so the brand, offer and next step remain clear on a small screen.

Prioritize the first view

Place the brand logo, key product cluster, offer and primary CTA near the top so readers can understand the message before exploring the supporting content.

Connect lifestyle and product

Use lifestyle imagery to establish context and product imagery to show what is being promoted. Keep backgrounds and hero treatments visually connected.

Preserve the brand

Balance vendor identity with ADI’s email structure through consistent spacing, typography, color and calls to action.

Production craft

Give every product a clear presentation.

Photoshop retouching, product image cleanup, cropping and resizing help different vendor assets work together in one email. Background choices support the hero and keep the transition into body content cohesive.

From design to campaign

Carry the design into email production.

Most of this email work was produced in Marketo. I also have experience using HubSpot for templates and marketing emails, adapting the visual content to the available email modules.

My contribution

  • Layout, visual hierarchy and CTA placement
  • Vendor branding and promotional graphics
  • Product retouching, image crops and optimized exports
  • Marketo campaign content and template-based production
  • Creative review and revisions with marketing partners
